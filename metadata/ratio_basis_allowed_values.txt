unknown

Known purified amplicon molar ratios are the most direct comparison with full-length 16S read percentages. gDNA mass, cell count, CFU, and culture-volume ratios are biological/input ratios and can differ from sequencing read percentages because of DNA extraction efficiency, 16S copy number, primer/PCR bias, and survival/culturability.
